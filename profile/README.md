# The movement for public AI

Public AI is first of all an idea: that AI is and can be a form of **public infrastructure** like highways, parks, libraries, water, electricity, and the Internet itself. The **movement for public AI**, or **Public AI** (capitalized), is a global movement committed to building public AI. It is not a single organization but a union of independent projects and contributors working toward a shared vision.

## What We Do

We coordinate around specific projects, including:

Tools and Infrastructure -
- **Public inference** - The [_Public AI Inference Utility_](http://github.com/forpublicai/publicai.co) is a pilot making public AI models more accessible and demonstrating real user demand for public AI. The [_Swiss Public Inference Utility_](http://github.com/forpublicai/publicai.ch) is a nation-level approach to this.
- **Public access in libraries** – [Drop-in AI makerspaces](https://github.com/forpublicai/libraries-kiosk) in public libraries.  
- **Fully open models** - Supporting fully open-source models like Apertus, Olmo, SEA-LION, and Salamandra; projects like [_Airbus for AI_](airbusforai.org) promoting multilateral collaboration toward a public-private frontier-scale model.

Coordination and Education -
- **Movement building** - the [_Public AI Network_](https://github.com/forpublicai/publicai.network) is a community of builders and advocates supporting one another in the movement towards more public AI.
- **Public writing** - [Whitepapers](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bcCPdRHyUGFB23--6wn4j1f9mRgdG2QF/view) and [handbooks](https://www.aspendigital.org/project/ai-benchmarks/) to inform policy and advocacy work; contests for [better fiction about AI futures](https://protopianprize.com/).
- **Benchmarks** – Educational resources on AI [benchmarks](https://www.aspendigital.org/report/benchmarks-101/) and evaluations; platforms like [_Weval_](https://weval.org/) for collaborative benchmarking.
- **Data** - [_Data flywheels_](https://nickmvincent.github.io/paidf_consultation/) for gathering usage data to improve public AI, and an _AI Commons_ of well-provenanced public knowledge for training and grounding AI systems.
